Most transfer apps are fast. Playlisty is careful.

Music catalogues are messy. There can be multiple versions, remasters, edits, live recordings and region-specific releases. Playlisty is built to help you choose the right Apple Music match instead of silently swapping tracks or skipping them.

Better matching

Prioritise sound quality, artwork or your own preferences when several Apple Music matches are possible.

No silent mistakes

If there is not an exact match, Playlisty does not just substitute or skip without checking with you.

Built for speed

Move large Spotify libraries quickly, including thousands of tracks, without turning the process into a weekend project.

Bring your Spotify library with you.

Import Spotify playlists, liked songs, albums and favourite artists into Apple Music. Keep playlist descriptions, choose how albums are transferred, and make the move feel like a move — not a rebuild.

Check the matches before they hit your library.

Playlisty scans Apple Music for the best available version of each track, then gives you control where the obvious answer is not obvious at all.
